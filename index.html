Welcome to the study

In this study you will compare everyday situations and rate how relevant one situation is to another. There are no right or wrong answers.

You will read one target situation, followed by eight other situations presented one at a time. For each, select a relevance rating from 1 to 7. You may also rate your confidence and explain your reasoning.
